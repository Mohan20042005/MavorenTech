What affects the price of a business website?

A website’s price depends on four things: how many pages it has, which features it needs, who supplies the content, and what happens after launch.

Pages: a one-page landing page is the lightest option. Mavoren’s starting prices run from ₹2,999 for a landing page to ₹14,999 for an e-commerce website.

Features: WhatsApp integration is part of every website package, while maps, enquiry forms and catalogues depend on the package. Carts, payment gateways, logins and dashboards add work and are quoted separately.

Content: clear photos, prices and text from you keep a project moving. Additional pages beyond a package are charged extra.

After launch: domain, hosting and payment gateway charges are separate unless your quote says otherwise, and maintenance is a monthly package starting at ₹999.

A useful first step is to write down the one thing you want visitors to do, such as call, book or order. Every feature should support that action.

Guide · Last updated 19 November 2026

What a restaurant or café website needs

A restaurant or café website has one job: help a hungry customer decide and act. These are the essentials.

A digital menu with categories and prices, so customers do not need to ask. Our Taj Restaurant site in Erode uses a category-tabbed menu with prices.

Location and directions: a map link and a call button on every page that matters.

A booking or enquiry route that finishes on WhatsApp. The Taj Restaurant site hands table reservations to WhatsApp, and Fab Café in Palladam takes custom cake enquiries the same way.

Current offers and real photos, updated when your menu changes.

A layout designed for the phone first, with large tap targets.

Mavoren’s Restaurant / Cafe Website package starts at ₹6,999. Online ordering and a payment gateway are not included in the basic package.

Guide · Last updated 19 November 2026

Landing page or full website: which should you start with?

The right choice depends on how many things you want to promote.

Choose a landing page (from ₹2,999) when you are promoting one offer or service. It is a single page with up to five content sections, WhatsApp integration and basic SEO.

Choose a business website (from ₹6,999) when you need separate pages such as About, Services and Contact, with up to five pages in total.

Choose an e-commerce website (from ₹14,999) when you are selling products online. It covers up to 25 products with a cart and checkout.

If you are unsure, the package finder on this site suggests a starting point, and you can estimate the starting price before you send an enquiry.
